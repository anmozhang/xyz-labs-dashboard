What changes when you withdraw

What happens on the 1st payout

  • Profit minus the 1% buffer is paid at 70%.–
  • The max-loss floor moves from $9,400 to $10,000 and never moves back down.
  • The payout counts as 1 of 4 toward the 75% profit share.
  • Paid weekly, minimum 100 USDT per withdrawal.
  • Paid-out profit still counts toward the 30% target for the wider daily loss limit.

Payout rules

  1. Daily loss works together with the floorThe most you can lose in a day is the smaller of 3% ($300) or your balance above the $10,000 floor. Balance $10,100 → you can lose up to $100. Balance $10,400 → you can lose up to $300.
  2. Request your 1st payout any timeNo minimum trading days, no waiting period after funding, no minimum profit. Minimum withdrawal is 100 USDT, counted before the profit split.
  3. Weekly payoutsPayouts are processed once a week.
  4. Paid-out profit counts toward 30%Profit you have already withdrawn still counts toward unlocking the 5% daily loss limit.
  5. Floating losses countLimits are checked on equity. If an open position's loss takes equity to the floor, the account breaches before the trade closes.
Example: after your 1st payout on a $10,000 account, your balance is $10,100. Today you can lose at most $100. Make $300 more, and tomorrow you can use the full $300 daily limit.
